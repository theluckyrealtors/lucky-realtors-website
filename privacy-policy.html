Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction & Purpose

THE LUCKY REALTORS ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, process, and safeguard personal information when you visit our website https://www.theluckyrealtors.com/ (the "Website"), interact with our property listings, submit buyer requirements, or register as a vendor/partner.

This policy is framed in accordance with applicable Indian laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 along with relevant rules.

2. Scope of this Policy

This policy applies to all visitors, prospective buyers, landowners, property vendors, and registered users interacting with our platform across India, Dubai, and global jurisdictions. By using our website or submitting information through our forms, you consent to the data practices described herein.

3. Personal Information We Collect

We collect only necessary information required to facilitate real estate advisory, land monetization, Joint Ventures (JV), and property transactions:

  • Contact & Identification Details: Full name, email address, phone numbers, and country codes provided via enquiries or registration.
  • Property & Requirement Details: Property titles, locations, budgets, land sizes, zoning preferences (e.g., Agriculture, R-Zone, NAINA, Industrial), and Google Maps location links.
  • Account & Authentication Information: Registered user IDs, firm/agency names, and login credentials maintained securely for vendor dashboards.
  • Communication Records: Details regarding enquiries submitted through WhatsApp integrations, telephone calls, or direct email correspondence.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your personal data is processed for legitimate business and real-estate transaction purposes, including:

  • Connecting buyers and investors with verified property opportunities and landowners.
  • Processing and reviewing vendor property submissions and buyer requirement listings.
  • Facilitating administrative verification, approvals, and user dashboard management.
  • Communicating regarding property updates, advisory meetings, and transactional requirements via phone, email, or WhatsApp.

5. Data Storage & Backend Integration

Our website utilizes Google Apps Script and secure Google Sheets databases to manage user accounts, property listings, and enquiry submissions. While Google provides robust cloud infrastructure, transmission over the internet carries inherent security risks.

6. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Information is disclosed solely:

  • To facilitate direct property connections between verified buyers, landowners, and our leadership board.
  • With trusted hosting providers and technical infrastructure platforms necessary to operate our website.
  • When required by law, court order, or governmental regulations in India.

7. Data Security Safeguards

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or alteration of personal data. Access to administrative records is restricted to authorized personnel.

8. Your Rights under Indian Law

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) framework, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Withdraw consent for future data processing by contacting us directly.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites, maps, or partner services (such as Google Maps or WhatsApp). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such external websites.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. The updated version will be posted directly on this page with a revised revision date.
